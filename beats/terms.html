POTENT Beats Agreements

Version 1.0. Plain-English terms. The version you accept, and the time you accept it, are saved with your purchase or application.

Part A — Exclusive Purchase Agreement (buyers)

1. What you are buying. POTENT Beats sells the master of a beat, exclusively, to one buyer. You are buying the exclusive rights to the master recording of the instrumental, as described in the "Rights package" on the beat's listing. There are no leases. The listing is part of this agreement. If the listing and this page disagree about what is granted, the listing's rights package controls.

2. Exclusive means one buyer. The seller will not sell or license the same beat to anyone else after your purchase completes, and the store removes the beat. Exclusivity starts when your payment is confirmed. If two people try to buy at once, the first confirmed payment wins and the other is refunded automatically.

3. What is and is not transferred. Unless the listing's rights package says so in words, buying a beat does not transfer ownership of (a) the underlying composition (the notes, melody and arrangement), (b) the sound recording or master of the instrumental, or (c) publishing or songwriting shares in the instrumental. You own what you create on top of it, such as your vocals and lyrics. Splits for songwriting or publishing, if any, must be agreed in writing separately.

4. Samples and third-party material. The seller must disclose samples or third-party material on the listing. If the listing discloses any, you are responsible for any clearance the listing says is still needed.

5. What you receive. The files listed under "What you get" on the listing, through a download link shown after payment. Exclusive purchases include up to two revisions (two rounds of changes requested from the producer). Keep your download safe; you are responsible for storing the files.

6. Price, fees, taxes. The price on the listing is what you pay at checkout. Taxes and payment-processing costs, if any, are shown or recorded separately on your receipt.

7. Refunds and disputes. Digital files are delivered immediately, so sales are final except where the law requires otherwise, if the seller cannot deliver, or if the seller did not have the rights it sold. Contact POTENT with your Stripe receipt. If a purchase is refunded, the exclusive is taken off sale until POTENT reviews it, and your right to use the beat ends if the refund covers the full price.

8. Your promises. You will not resell or give away the instrumental files by themselves, claim you made the instrumental, or use the beat in a way the law forbids.

9. Records. POTENT keeps your order, the agreement version you accepted, and the time, so both sides can check what was agreed.

10. Splits shown anywhere on the site. Any percentage split described on this site is a financial or contractual term for the revenue the listing or agreement names. It is not a statement that anyone owns that percentage of a copyright.

Part B — Seller Agreement (outside producers)

1. Approval. POTENT must approve you before you can list anything. Every beat you submit is reviewed before it can be bought. POTENT may reject, pause or remove a listing.

2. Your promise about rights. You promise that you own, or are legally authorized to grant, every right you offer on each listing, and that your disclosure of samples and third-party material is complete and true. You will not offer exclusive rights you do not control. If you list a beat you have already sold or licensed exclusively elsewhere, that is a breach.

3. Exclusive means exclusive. After a sale you will not sell, license or reuse that beat in a way that conflicts with the buyer's rights.

4. How you are paid. Your share of each sale is the percentage POTENT agrees with you in writing when it approves you. It is calculated on the sale amount after taxes and payment-processing costs, and is credited to your wallet after the hold period shown there. Money is paid out only after POTENT confirms the sale is final and the payment provider has settled the funds. Refunds and chargebacks reduce your balance by your share of the refunded amount. Payout requests need the payout code that POTENT controls.

5. Files. Files you upload are stored privately until a buyer pays. After an exclusive sells, POTENT keeps the delivered files available to that buyer, even if you later remove your account.

6. Percentages. Any artist / producer / platform percentage shown by POTENT applies only to the specific revenue stream named in writing for that deal. It does not turn the beat's purchase price into three separate payments unless that is written for the listing.
