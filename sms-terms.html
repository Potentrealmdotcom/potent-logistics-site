Text Messages & Booking Updates

POTENT OPERATIONS LLC (doing business as POTENT Logistics). How our booking text messages work and how you agree to receive them.

POTENT Booking Texts

When you book a service with POTENT OPERATIONS LLC by phone and give us your mobile number on that call, you agree to receive text messages about your booking — confirmations, appointment reminders, and follow-up about that booking — from POTENT OPERATIONS LLC at the number you provided.

Our booking calls are recorded, and your agreement to receive these texts is given verbally on that call. These are booking messages only — we do not send marketing texts under this program.

Message frequency varies with your booking. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase. Questions: call (912) 914-2166 or email info@potentworld.online.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy.
