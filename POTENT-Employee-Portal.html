Sales Playbook · Chapters 4–18

Selling the POTENT Way.

Chapter 7
The POTENT Calling System

Southern hospitality. Serious sales. Real relationships. Every call: Open → Discover → Listen → Dig → Qualify → Solve → Close → Follow Up.

Opening Script

"Hey, this is [NAME] with POTENT Operations. How are you doing today?" — Let them answer. Then: "I'll keep this quick — I know you've got a business to run. Did I catch you with about 30 seconds?"
"The reason I'm calling is we help businesses when freight needs to move quickly, when regular transportation can't cover something, or when they need a reliable option. Out of curiosity — how are you currently handling those situations?" Then STOP. Listen.

Objection Responses

"We already have a carrier." → "That makes sense. Most companies we work with already have providers. I'm curious — what happens when those carriers are unavailable or something urgent comes up?"
"Send me information." → "Absolutely. Before I do — what type of transportation challenges does your company usually run into?"
"Your price is too high." → "Is it higher than what you're currently paying, or higher than you expected?" Then: "How important is the delivery timeline?"
"I need to think about it." → "Of course. Usually there's something specific they're considering. What would you say is the biggest thing?"
"We don't need anything right now." → "I understand. Problems usually happen unexpectedly. Do you have a backup option in place if something urgent comes up?"
Chapter 10
Closing — Confirm Before You Close
Problem confirmed: "The biggest challenge is ___, correct?"
Impact confirmed: "When that happens, it creates ___ for your team, correct?"
Goal confirmed: "You would want a partner that can ___, correct?"
Direct Close: "Based on everything we discussed, is there any reason we shouldn't move forward?" — Then be quiet. Silence is a skill.

Driver Protocol · Chapters 21 & 32–33

Driver Operations.

Everything you need is in the POTENT OS Driver App tab. Open it on your phone browser — no download needed.

Before Every Job

  • Check active jobs in Driver App
  • Confirm pickup address and customer phone
  • Perform vehicle inspection before driving
  • Update status to En Route when you leave
  • Call customer when 10 minutes away

At Pickup

  • Update status to Arrived
  • Photograph BOL before loading — tap 📸 Photo/BOL in Driver App
  • Confirm correct freight, quantity, condition — document damage before loading
  • Secure freight properly. Never overload vehicle.
  • Update to In Transit when loaded and leaving

At Delivery

  • Update status to Delivered
  • Photograph Proof of Delivery
  • Collect payment if cash job — count it, confirm amount
  • Report any damage to Owner BEFORE leaving site

If Something Goes Wrong

Breakdown: Get somewhere safe → notify Owner → notify customer → protect freight.
Accident: Stop → check injuries → call 911 if needed → call Owner before moving vehicle → document everything → do NOT admit fault.
Freight damaged: Stop handling it → photograph → notify Owner → notify customer → do NOT promise refunds.
Wrong address or job changes: STOP. Verify through Owner before proceeding. Document everything.
Compliance & Safety · Chapters 32–33

Safety & Compliance.

If you're unsure whether a job is legal, safe, insured, or within POTENT's authority — STOP and contact the Owner.

Zero Impairment Rule

No weed. No alcohol. No intoxicating substances before or during driving.

Once you're behind the wheel representing POTENT, the standard changes. We're responsible for our customers, their freight, other people on the road, our employees, our insurance, and our company.

Off the clock = your personal life. Behind the wheel for POTENT = zero impairment. No exceptions. No debate.

If you are not fit to drive: DO NOT DRIVE. Notify management immediately. There is no job, deadline, or paycheck worth risking someone's life.

Before Accepting Any Job

  • Correct service and equipment for the job
  • Pickup and delivery locations confirmed
  • Freight described — no hazardous or prohibited materials
  • Vehicle load capacity not exceeded
  • Required insurance and authority in place
Never accept a job outside your authority, insurance, equipment, or legal requirements.

Escalation Levels

Level 1 — You handle it: Normal customer/service issue within your authority.
Level 2 — Manager: Pricing exceptions, disputes, major delays, unusual requests.
Level 3 — Owner immediately: Accident, injury, freight loss/damage, legal threat, insurance issue, compliance issue, anything you're unsure about.
When in doubt — escalate. Always.
People first. Freight second. Customer informed. Owner informed. Everything documented.
