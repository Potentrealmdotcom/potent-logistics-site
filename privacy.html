Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Who We Are and What This Covers

POTENT LOGISTICS LLC and POTENT OPERATIONS LLC ("POTENT," "we") provide delivery, junk removal and hauling services and the POTENT OS software platform. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our websites, apps, text messages, calls and services (the "Services"), how we use it, and your choices. It applies to customers who book with us, drivers and carriers, dispatch partners, and software customers and their staff. When a company that licenses POTENT OS uses it to serve its own customers, that company decides how that information is used, and we process it on its behalf.

2. Information We Collect

Contact and account details: name, phone, email, company, addresses, logins and password hashes. Job details: pickup and drop-off addresses, item descriptions, photos and videos you or our drivers take, notes, signatures, proof-of-delivery and bill-of-lading records, quotes, payments and invoices. Business verification: USDOT and MC numbers, EIN, insurance and bond documents, identification documents and selfies for driver and partner verification. Location: your address, and for drivers, device location while a job is active or while the app is open, used for dispatch, tracking and safety. Payments: processed by Stripe; we receive confirmations and limited card details (brand, last four digits), not full card numbers. Device and usage data: IP address, device and browser type, pages and actions, login times and approximate location from IP, cookies and similar technologies. Communications: messages, call recordings or notes, support requests. Terms acceptance records: the version accepted, time, and device and network address.

3. Precise Location

When you use our booking pages, ask for an automated route estimate, or track a dispatch vehicle, we may collect your device's precise location, with your permission. We use it only to check distance from our base, work out road miles and arrival times, and coordinate the job. You can turn location off in your device settings; booking by typing the address still works.

4. Job Photos and Property Documentation

Our drivers photograph the site and the load at pickup, during loading, and at delivery, disposal and completion. Photos carry timestamps and may carry location data. We use them to confirm the job was done, support billing, and resolve claims and chargebacks. Photos may show your property and belongings.

5. Identity Verification

To meet cargo, disposal and fraud-prevention requirements, we may ask to see, and may keep a copy of, government-issued ID (such as a driver's license) from a customer or authorized agent at pickup or delivery. We use it only to confirm authority to release or receive items and to protect both sides from unauthorized removal or illegal disposal. We do not sell it, and we keep it only as long as needed for those purposes and any claim.

6. How We Use Information

To give quotes, schedule, perform and document jobs; take payment and send receipts; send confirmations, arrival times and tracking links; verify partners and drivers and prevent fraud; keep records for claims and disputes; provide customer support; run, secure, debug and improve the Services; comply with law and enforce our terms; and, if you agree, send promotions. We may use photos and job records in anonymized or aggregated form to improve estimating tools, and we do not use your photos for advertising without your permission.

7. Texts and Calls

If you give us your mobile number, you agree we may text and call about your job and account. Message frequency varies with your job. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts, HELP for help. Marketing texts are sent only with your separate consent, which is not a condition of buying anything.

8. Automated Tools and AI

Some tools use automated systems, including AI, to read documents, translate text, geocode addresses, and estimate load size and weight from photos. These produce estimates that our staff review before a quote is final. We do not use them to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you without human review.

9. How We Share Information

With service providers that act for us under contract: hosting and database (Supabase, Netlify), payments (Stripe), email and messaging delivery, mapping and routing (OpenStreetMap, the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder), document and AI processing providers, and analytics. With our drivers, helpers, vendors and disposal facilities as needed to do your job. With a customer's or partner's own company when we act as its service provider. With insurers, claims administrators, factoring companies and professional advisers where needed. With authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety and property. In a merger, financing or sale of our business. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential storage to keep you signed in and remember settings, and limited analytics to understand site use. You can block cookies in your browser, but parts of the Services may not work.

11. How Long We Keep Information

We keep job and payment records for as long as needed for the job, for tax, insurance, claims and legal purposes (generally up to seven years), and verification documents only as long as needed for compliance. We delete or anonymize information we no longer need.

12. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, per-company data separation, role-based access and audit records. No system is perfectly secure. Keep your password confidential and tell us if you suspect unauthorized access.

13. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, to receive a copy, or to opt out of marketing, by emailing potentlogistics@pm.me. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, including the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing (we do not sell or share), and to appeal a denial. We will verify your request and respond within the time the law requires. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. If you are a customer of a company using POTENT OS, contact that company first.

14. Children

The Services are not directed to people under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Driver Location and Safety Data

Drivers who use our app agree that location is collected during active work and shared with dispatch, and with the customer for tracking links for that job. Drivers can see when tracking is on. We keep location history only as long as needed for job records, safety, payment disputes and legal requirements.

16. Changes to This Policy

We will post updates here with a new date, and tell you of material changes through the Services or by email. This version is effective October 8, 2026.
