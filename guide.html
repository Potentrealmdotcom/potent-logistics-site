How booking works

  1. Get an instant quote. Enter the pickup and drop-off. We measure the real road route.
  2. Add photos and measurements. Required to book. No photos, no measurements, no booking.
  3. Review your price. You see the total before you commit. No surprise fees.
  4. Accept the terms and pay. Cash at the job (10% off) or card online.
  5. Track your driver. Live truck location on your tracking link.
  6. Job done. Photos of pickup and delivery are saved to your job, and you get your receipt.

Our truck

16 ft box truck. Maximum cargo weight 4,500 lbs. Interior about 190″ long, with a door opening of 87″ wide by 72″ high. Anything that will not fit is flagged before you book, and larger jobs are split into more than one trip.

Price protection

Your confirmed price does not change unless the real job differs from what was booked (more items, more weight, larger size, a different location or blocked access). If that happens we stop, document it with photos, show you the revised scope and price, and only continue after you authorize it. If you decline, the job stays at the original scope.

Questions

Do I need to be there?

Items must be ready and accessible before the driver arrives. For junk removal we go inside, so someone needs to show us what goes.

Why do you need photos?

So the price is real. Photos and measurements let us confirm the truck fits your load and quote it correctly the first time.

Is the price final?

Yes, unless the actual job is different from what was booked. We will never raise a price without showing you and getting your approval first.

How do I pay?

Cash (10% off, due before we leave) or card online. We do not take checks.

Can I set up a regular route?

Yes. Dedicated and recurring routes are priced above. Call (770) 648-4228 to set one up.

Do you move large or very heavy items?

Yes, within our 4,500 lb limit. Heavy items carry a weight charge shown in your quote.
