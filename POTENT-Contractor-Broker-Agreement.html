1. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

POTENT Logistics LLC operates as an independent contract motor carrier, not as an employee of the Counterparty. POTENT Logistics LLC maintains its own operating authority, equipment, insurance, and control over the manner and means of performing transportation services. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or joint venture between the parties. POTENT Logistics LLC is solely responsible for its own tax obligations, including self-employment tax, and will receive a Form 1099-NEC for services rendered where applicable, not a W-2.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

POTENT Logistics LLC agrees to provide transportation services within the State of Georgia, consistent with its DOT registration and operating authority. Services are limited to intrastate transportation unless otherwise agreed in writing. Each load, shipment, or engagement shall be governed by a separate rate confirmation, bill of lading, or work order referencing this Agreement.

3. INSURANCE

POTENT Logistics LLC maintains general liability insurance of $750,000 and cargo liability insurance of $100,000, and will provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) upon request. Counterparty is responsible for verifying that this coverage is adequate for the specific freight, value, or risk profile of goods being transported and may request additional cargo coverage limits be arranged separately if higher-value freight is involved.

4. RATES & PAYMENT TERMS

Rates for each shipment shall be agreed upon in writing (rate confirmation, invoice, or work order) prior to the transportation of goods. Payment terms are net 15 days from delivery and receipt of a valid invoice and proof of delivery, unless otherwise agreed in writing. Late payments beyond 30 days may accrue a 1.5% monthly late fee.

5. BROKER-SPECIFIC TERMS (if applicable)

If Counterparty is acting as a licensed property broker, Counterparty represents and warrants that it holds valid FMCSA broker authority and, where applicable, an active BMC-84 bond or BMC-85 trust fund. Counterparty agrees to provide accurate load information, including commodity, weight, pickup/delivery windows, and any special handling requirements, prior to dispatch. Double-brokering of loads tendered under this Agreement without POTENT Logistics LLC's prior written consent is strictly prohibited and constitutes a material breach.

6. INDEMNIFICATION

Each party agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the other party, its officers, employees, and agents from any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including reasonable attorney's fees) arising from that party's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement. This provision survives termination of this Agreement.

7. CARGO CLAIMS

Any cargo loss or damage claims must be reported in writing within 9 months of delivery, consistent with the Carmack Amendment where applicable to interstate commerce, or applicable Georgia state law for intrastate shipments. POTENT Logistics LLC's liability for cargo loss or damage is limited to the lesser of the actual value of the goods or the cargo insurance limits stated above, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

8. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice. Termination does not affect obligations for shipments already tendered, in transit, or completed prior to the termination date.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary business information, rates, customer lists, and operational data shared in the course of this relationship, and not disclose such information to third parties without written consent.

10. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia. Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Rockdale County, Georgia, or through mediation/arbitration if both parties agree in writing.

11. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement, together with any rate confirmations or work orders issued under it, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations or agreements, written or oral, relating to the subject matter herein.
