How the resale royalties work

A permanent 5% royalty on every qualifying verified resale. Example: one verified resale for $1,000 —

Reseller / current sellerReceives 95% of the resale price$950
Original creator2.5% permanent resale royalty$25
Original buyer1.5% permanent resale royalty$15
POTENT1% platform royalty$10
Total distributed$1,000
  1. The creator receives the original creator royalty on every qualifying resale.
  2. The original buyer continues receiving their royalty, even after selling the clothing.
  3. Every subsequent owner can resell the item subject to the Vault's rules, including the 180-day holding period.
  4. The current seller receives the remaining proceeds — 95%.
  5. POTENT receives its 1% platform royalty.

The 5% royalty pool is split permanently between the original creator (2.5%), the original buyer (1.5%), and POTENT (1%). Percentages are illustrated before any separately applicable taxes or transaction costs. Retail/MSRP value is not a guarantee of cash resale value or profit.
