What is inside

Everything below is included in every plan. The plan only sets your size.

Offline-first app that installs like a real app

  • Add to Home Screen on iPhone and Android. No app store, no store fees.
  • Jobs save on the device with no signal and sync the moment you are back online.
  • Blue "syncing" and green "synced" banners so nothing is ever lost.

Quoting and booking

  • Live pricing calculator while you are on the phone, with voice-activated quoting.
  • Real address autocomplete and real road-mile routing.
  • Photos and measurements gate, so no job is booked without them.
  • Cash discount, card payments, and booking confirmation emails.

Job management

  • Full status flow from New to Completed, with filters, search and bulk updates.
  • Job clock with arrival, loading, driving, unloading and completion times.
  • Scope-difference protection: drivers report it, the office sets the price, the customer authorizes.
  • Route legs per job, trips, deadhead and return miles.
  • Terms versioning: every booking records which terms were accepted and when.

Profit protection

  • Profit Guard on every quote: miles, gas, profit, and whether the job covers your monthly bills.
  • Heavy-item pricing so you never eat dump weight.
  • Capacity review when a load is over your truck limit.

Drivers

  • Separate driver login with only the driver screens.
  • Live GPS every 10 seconds, with a tracking link for your customer.
  • Photo upload, fuel log by state for IFTA, one-tap navigation and an SOS button.

Fleet and compliance

  • Trucks, maintenance and expiry warnings.
  • CDL and medical-card tracking so nothing lapses.
  • Hours-of-service awareness and dispatch that will not assign a truck with expired maintenance.

Vendors and disposal

  • Vendor command center for containers, overflow trucks, labor and specialty services.
  • Disposal facility records with verification status. Anything not re-verified in 30 days is flagged.

Customers and money

  • Customer portal and tracking, recurring routes and pre-paid wallets.

Sales tools

  • Leads board, call logging and follow-up scheduling, pipeline and leaderboard.
  • Company-branded email so quotes and receipts go out under your name.

Your data, your company

  • Every company is completely separate. You only ever see your own data.
  • Export your own data any time, and import trucks, drivers and customers from a spreadsheet.

14 languages

Getting set up

You never touch a database or a server. After you apply, your company account is created and you land on a Get Started checklist. POTENT can set it up for you with your permission, and you can block that access any time.

  1. Apply. Tell us your fleet size. We review applications personally.
  2. Pay once, or use Flex Pay. Your account opens when the license is paid.
  3. Add your company. USDOT and MC numbers, logo, colors, and the email your customers reply to.
  4. Bring your data. Import trucks and drivers from a spreadsheet, Samsara or Motive.
  5. Set your rates and run a test quote.
  6. Connect payouts. Your customers pay you directly into your own account.

Questions

Is there a monthly software fee?

No per-truck or per-user software fee. Support is optional and billed monthly.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Jobs save on the device and sync when the connection returns.

Who owns my data?

You do. Each company is kept completely separate, and you can export your own data at any time.

Can I bring my existing trucks and drivers?

Yes. Import from a spreadsheet, or pull them from Samsara or Motive with your own token.

What happens when I grow?

Move up to the next plan. Each plan allows a set number of trucks, drivers and dispatch profiles. Over 300 trucks gets a custom quote.

Can POTENT set it up for me?

Yes. With your permission, we can enter your account for a limited time to set it up. It is logged and you can block it.
