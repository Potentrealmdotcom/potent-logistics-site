LEGIT Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026. Operator: POTENT OPERATIONS LLC, 1820 Highway 20, Ste 114 #440, Conyers, GA. Privacy contact: potentrealm@proton.me.

What we collect

  • Account: email, password (stored only as a scrambled hash), first name, age, gender and who you are interested in.
  • Profile: photos, bio, prompts, body type, intent, Tonight's Intention tags, and your preferences.
  • Approximate location: if you allow it, a rounded location (about 7 miles). We never store or show your exact location.
  • Verification: identity verification is run by Stripe Identity. LEGIT receives only the result (verified or not), the date, and a Stripe reference. LEGIT does not store your ID images or selfie. Stripe processes them under its own policy. If Stripe returns your date of birth, we use it only to confirm you are 18 or older.
  • Messages and activity: your messages, likes, matches, reports, blocks, strikes, and Love Board posts, comments and reactions.
  • Payments: processed by Stripe. We keep the amount, date and Stripe reference. We never see your card number.
  • Technical: basic logs such as last-seen time and security records.

How we use it

To run the service, verify you, show your profile to other verified members, create matches, deliver messages, prevent scams and abuse, enforce the Terms, process payments, answer reports, and meet legal duties. Messages are scanned automatically for phone numbers, emails, social handles and similar contact details, which are hidden from the other person. Authorized LEGIT administrators may read messages when needed for safety, fraud prevention, reports, or legal reasons. Each time an administrator opens a private chat, it is recorded in an audit log.

Who sees your information

Other verified members see your profile (photos, name, age, bio, tags, approximate distance). We use service providers to run LEGIT: Supabase (database and hosting of data), Netlify (website hosting), and Stripe (verification and payments). If you use optional AI features, limited text such as profile details may be sent to Google's Gemini service to produce suggestions, such as opener ideas. We do not sell your personal information or your messages. We may disclose information when the law requires it, to respond to valid legal process, or to protect people from harm.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in Profile at any time. That removes your profile, matches, messages and Love Board activity. We may keep payment records, and safety records such as strikes and reports, for as long as needed for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention and disputes.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, turn off location, block and report people, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. Email potentrealm@proton.me and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

Passwords are hashed. Access to administrator tools is restricted and logged. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and never share it.

Children

LEGIT is only for adults 18 and over. We remove accounts of anyone found to be under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new date here and tell you in the app when the change is material.
