LEGIT Terms & Privacy

Draft. This plain-language draft must be reviewed by a lawyer before real users are charged. Last updated Oct 2026.

1. Who can use LEGIT

You must be 18 or older. You must verify your identity with a government ID and a selfie before you can use the app. Fake IDs, other people's IDs, or lying about your age lead to an immediate ban.

2. How verification works

Verification is done by Stripe Identity. LEGIT keeps only the result (verified or not), the date, and a Stripe reference number. We do not store your ID images. Stripe handles them under its own privacy policy.

3. Price

Your first 24 hours after verification are free. After that, access costs a one-time payment of $39.99. There is no subscription and no premium tier. Payments are handled by Stripe. All sales are final unless the law requires otherwise, or we choose to refund you.

4. Messages are scanned and may be reviewed

To fight scams and keep people safe, every message you send is automatically scanned for phone numbers, emails, social handles and similar contact details. Those parts are hidden from the other person. Authorized LEGIT administrators can read messages when a message is flagged, when a report is filed, or when needed to investigate abuse, fraud or safety issues. Each time an administrator opens a private chat, it is written to an audit log. We do not sell your messages.

5. Strikes and bans

Trying to share contact details gets a strike. Three strikes lock your account automatically. An administrator can review and reverse a ban. We can also remove accounts for harassment, hate, threats, scams, nudity you did not have consent to share, or anything illegal.

6. Matches and openers

A match disappears after 24 hours if no one sends a real opener. First messages must be more than a bare greeting.

7. Your profile and location

Your exact location is never shown. We store a rounded location (about 7 miles) so we can show approximate distance. Photos you upload are shown to other verified members.

8. Love Board

A Love Board post is only public after both people confirm it. You can remove your post at any time by contacting us.

9. Safety

LEGIT does not run background checks and cannot guarantee anyone's intentions. Meet in public places, tell a friend where you are going, and never send money to someone you meet here. Report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.

10. Your data

You can delete your account in your Profile at any time. This removes your profile, matches and messages. We may keep payment records and safety records (such as strikes and reports) as the law or fraud prevention requires. Tell us at the email below about any privacy request.

11. Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will tell you in the app. Contact: potentrealm@proton.me.
