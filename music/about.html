Help & FAQ

How it works

What is an exclusive master beat?

You buy the beat outright. Once it sells, it is removed from the store and no one else can buy it. POTENT only sells masters, there are no leases.

What do I get after paying?

Your files appear on the confirmation page right away and stay in your library if you have an account. Sign in with the same email to keep everything in one place.

What are digital downloads and preorders?

Sample packs, presets, stems, albums and more, sold directly by creators. Preorders are charged now and the download unlocks on the release date shown.

Can I get a refund?

Digital goods are delivered instantly, so refunds are handled case by case. Use the form below and include your order email. Approved refunds go back to your card.

What does POTENT charge?

Buyers never pay a platform fee. POTENT keeps 1% of the eligible sale amount (the sale minus tax). Exclusive beat sellers receive at least 91% of that amount and the remainder is documented as unassigned, never paid to anyone silently. Card-processing fees are Stripe's and are shown separately.

How do I sell?

Apply as a seller for exclusive beats, or sign in to the Studio to upload songs, videos and downloads. POTENT reviews listings before they go live.

Something I bought is not mine to sell / copyright issue

Use the ⚑ report button on any listing. POTENT reviews every report and can take items down.
