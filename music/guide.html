For producers, songwriters, DJs and promoters

Producers: upload and price exclusive beats. Once a beat sells it is removed from the store. You get your own split for the master only if the artist's agreement says so.

Songwriters: you are paid from the publishing split, which is separate from the master. Make sure your name and share are on that sheet.

DJs and promoters: a promo deal pays you for the work you agreed to. It does not make you an owner of the song unless the agreement clearly says so. Ask for the deliverables, dates and payment in writing.
