Privacy notice

What we collect

  • Account: your email, name (optional) and a salted hash of your password. We never store your password itself.
  • Purchases: what you bought, the amount, and the agreement version and time you accepted. Card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers.
  • Activity you create: favorites, follows, playlists, and a listening history. You can turn history off or clear it any time in your account.
  • Messages: support messages and replies.

How it is used

  • To deliver what you bought, show your library and receipts, and keep sellers' records accurate.
  • To email you about your orders. Marketing email only happens if you opt in, and every message has its own unsubscribe link.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to review reports.

Who sees it

Sellers see sales of their own items, not your account details beyond what is needed to deliver. Payment processing is by Stripe. Our database is hosted with Supabase. If AI help is switched on, only the minimum project information needed for the answer you asked for is sent to Google's Gemini service, and nothing is sent when AI help is off.

Your choices

You can export your list preferences, turn off marketing email, turn off listening history, and ask us to close your account from the Help page. Records we must keep for financial or legal reasons are kept as the law requires.
