How licensing works

Exclusive beats

Each beat is sold once, to one buyer. The listing states exactly what the buyer receives (the rights package) and any samples or third-party material used. The buyer's saved Exclusive Purchase Agreement version and acceptance time appear on the receipt.

Buying a beat is not a transfer of anything the listing does not say. Ownership of the underlying composition and publishing stays with the seller unless the listing states otherwise.

Money split on a beat sale

The seller receives 91%. Whoever made the beat receives an 8% royalty. POTENT's fee is 1%. A payment split is a way of dividing money. It does not change who owns the music.

Downloads

Each download lists what the buyer may do with it. Read it before you pay.

Working with others on a song

Use Projects to record who worked on a song and how each type of income is divided. Master recording income and songwriting income are kept separate, and everyone named approves their own share.
